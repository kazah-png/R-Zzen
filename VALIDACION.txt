Zzen v0.2 - Validation report
27 October 2026 | Europe/Madrid | Native C++17 | Windows x64

CAUSES REPRODUCED
- The overnight v0.1 log showed promotion-menu navigation failures stopping
  the entire session, including keep-active.
- A saved screenshot showed an enabled BUILD $700 button on oil platform 5,
  read as BUILD sno. Construction repeatedly reopened without buying.
- Live testing also reproduced the game's hover font change making the
  price unreadable during a fresh pre-click check.

CORRECTIONS
- Additional targeted price reading; stable building-name/level recognition.
- Before clicking, verify the same building, level and enabled cash button.
  The initial price must be numeric. A readable changed price cancels input.
- Bounded menu recovery replaces fatal stops for ordinary navigation failures.
- Independent three-minute keep-active deadline, prioritized on a clear HUD.
- Bounded, cancellable Windows OCR reads and nonblocking session restarts.
- Retry transient read/input errors; show recovery/attention in the interface.
- Active sessions request Windows system/display wakefulness.
- Text-only feedback includes recovery/error counts and keep-active age.

LIMITS AND HISTORICAL EVIDENCE
- No finite test establishes perfect operation or guarantees against future
  game changes, disconnections, Windows lock or loss of foreground focus.
- This version does not reconnect Roblox or automate combat/premium purchases.
- Uncertain promotion outcomes preserve the pending record and suspend
  purchases; unknown screens do not authorize blind clicks or keep-active.
- Keep-active is approximately every three minutes, deferred until clear HUD.
- Historical v0.1 testing visually verified promotion 11 -> 12. Its overnight
  log recorded 12 -> 13 at 00:29:34, followed by purchases. These are not new
  full prestige cycles on v0.2; 13 -> 14 remains unverified on v0.2.
- The verified-upgrades UI counter undercounts successful purchases when the
  game closes the panel immediately. Closure alone is not counted as proof.
  Live upgrade toasts/building levels provide separate visual evidence.
- Session counters reset when a new session starts.

Only the app, English guide, README, license, notice, validation and checksum
are packaged. Personal logs, private screenshots and fixtures remain local.
